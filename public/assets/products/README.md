## Notes

- The three depth-camera model mappings are inferred from their left-to-right
  placement beside the 25 mm, 48 mm, and 100 mm variants on slide 11. The PPT
  does not embed the model number in the image metadata.
- `nrb-h1.png` and the slide 6 images are engineering concept renders. The PPT
  labels nRB-H1 dimensions, power, and related specifications as preliminary.
- Slides 9-11 are marked proprietary and confidential. These files are suitable
  for the internal demo, but external publication should be cleared by the
  product/content owner first.
